Answers

Frequently asked.

How do I pay and get access? +

Open the Telegram bot, choose a plan and pay in USDT (TRC-20). Your dashboard login is created automatically and sent to you in the chat, usually within a minute.

Which systems does it work on? +

The agent runs on Windows PCs. You control them from any browser, or from the HatchConnect desktop app, on Windows, Mac, iPad and phones.

Is it secure? +

Sessions are AES-256 encrypted end to end and work through firewalls without opening ports. Optional uninstall protection keeps the agent in place on machines you manage.

Can I remove the agent later? +

Yes. Uninstall is available at any time, and uninstall protection is entirely under your control from the dashboard.

How do I get support? +

Message us on Telegram. We answer setup, billing and technical questions directly in the chat.
