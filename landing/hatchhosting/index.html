Answers

Frequently asked.

How do I sign up and pay? +

Open the Telegram bot, choose a plan and pay in USDT (TRC-20). Your hosting account and panel login are created automatically and sent to you in the chat.

Do I need to be technical? +

No. The panel is built for beginners, with one-click WordPress, guided DNS steps and quick help throughout. SSL turns on by itself.

Can I use my own domain? +

Yes. Add your domain in the panel and it shows the exact records to point it here. Once it points, HTTPS turns on automatically.

Is my site backed up? +

Yes. Backups run automatically every night, and you can create or download your own at any time from the panel.

How do I get support? +

Message us on Telegram. We help with setup, your domain, email, billing and anything else, directly in the chat.
